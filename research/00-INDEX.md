## The ten most important lessons (all built into the plan doc)
See the **Starforge — Game Plan** doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/3h73quHF3TSQtZQe4m6QcP
1. Make the **first reset quick** and rewarding (Math and balance: balance targets).
2. **New systems every stage**, a few at a time (Overview: Systems by stage).
3. **Milestones remove chores** (Math and balance: reset layers).
4. Show the **next goal on screen**: teaser rails and the Ascent map (Game design: across the whole game).
5. **One big-number type everywhere** (CLAUDE.md rules 2 and 3).
6. **Offline progress**: none of the 8 games had it properly (Game design: across the whole game).
7. **Ceremony for rare rolls** (UI style: rarity table).
8. **Luck as a root** for huge odds, plus luck-immune Anomalies and a luck slider (Math and balance: rune maths).
9. **No waiting-only resources and no timers**; requirements are amounts (Game design: rules from the research).
10. **No pay-to-win, no premium currency, no pop-ups** (Game design: rules from the research).
