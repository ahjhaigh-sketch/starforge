# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (keep what you earn)
Save version 22: the real 1.2.0 playtest save (version 21, 16 hours, the first Galactic Rebirth) is a test fixture and loads with everything kept; a tenth of what it holds starts kept safe. Built from that playtest and its save, which showed what was wrong: the Void Shrine's auto-roll had spent all 2.1e17 Dark Matter ever earned, a Comet Fusion board was stuck for good, comet buffs had 1e79 seconds left, and Singularities after the first ran on passive Essence alone.
- **The safe tenth.** Automation (auto-buyers, rune auto-roll, Eternity, the Prism Wheel, Eternal Void) can never spend the last 10% of what you earn (`automation.keepShare`; `state.kept`, `automatedSpend`, `free`, `usable`). Your own purchases still can. Hover or tap a currency for what's kept safe and what spent it over the last few seconds.
- **Passive gains** from a layer's own milestone: Essence at 9 Nebulas, Dark Matter at 15 Singularities, Galaxy Cores at 4 Galactic Rebirths, Chronons at 4 Big Crunches (`BALANCE.passive`). Passive income is yours to spend and counts in lifetime totals, but not in run totals (`gainPassive`): counted there, it made every reset come round sooner (the simulator's first Big Crunch at 1 h 23 min instead of 8 h).
- **Galactic Rebirth** needs the anti-star at Protostar (Mirror depth 11) instead of 8 Black Hole stages; Galaxy Cores past 10,000 in one rebirth count at a square root (`galactic.softcapAt`).
- **Recommended** focus card (`src/app/recommend.ts`): a reset worth taking, "build up first" for a weak reset, the next Galactic step, an affordable tree, a megastructure stage or a better constellation. Reset screens warn when a reset pays under 1% of the layer's best (`bestGain.<layer>` stats).
- **New things start slowly:** the late ramp (`BALANCE.lateRamp`) makes Belt, Starforge, Deep Core, turret, ship, Spectral and Wyrm prices steeper every 100 levels past 300.
- **Comet Fusion** can't deadlock: a full board with no match takes the arriving comet as the third of a pair, or swaps out a leftover low comet.
- **Clearer panels:** a how-it-works strip (`ui/kit/HowTo`) on the Nursery, Tarot, Auras, Wyrms, Spire, Reactor, Rune Stars and Starships. The Nursery and Wyrms have pages; the Crossbreed book crosses from each recipe; Tarot shows its total and deals the best deck; Wyrms has Best team and Fuse all; Rune Stars has Star every shrine.
- **Deeper systems:** Auras get three slots, Polish ranks and families; the Nursery a Garden bonus; Spire banners boost what they name; the Weather tab a radar, forecast, auto-summon and Almanac; the Observatory Hint and Auto-draw (Star Chart half price); Alchemy recipe hints; the Index covers the whole game (18 pages); the Ascent shows perks.
- **More to build:** Constant Flow (Nebula Tree, Auto-Constants every second), Forge Heat, Core Pressure and Belt Current constants, Lucky Rocks (Belt), Purity (Starforge). Leviathans pay x10 scales on a first kill and toughen x1.8 a kill (was x2.5).
- **Graphics:** a machine for every engine you own orbits the star (`render/engineOrbit.ts`); Wyrms are dragons; a new Leviathan; moving megastructures; turning planets with more moonlets and stations; lit, cratered asteroids; twirling Spark Motes; a new Planet Defense battlefield.
- **Fixed:** comet buffs capped at 50 times their length; time away capped at a year; huge times read properly; the welcome-back screen names every currency; the Recommended card's class no longer collides with the tooltip style.

- **Interface.** UI size 80 to 130% (the root rem, so the sky is untouched); hide parts of the screen; reset animations on or off; ships-home pop-ups off by default. Menus: hide any but the Starforge, Nova Tree and Fusion; menus that run themselves tuck away once you've opened them (shown again from Settings, and always reachable from the Ascent). The engine list minimizes to one line once every engine has its auto-buyer on (Show opens it; Full, Compact or Minimized in Settings). Focus cards at the side: a system you just unlocked and the one or two you're closest to.
- **Medals and the Wardrobe.** 91 new medals (59 milestones, 14 combinations, 10 unlocks, 12 secrets found by poking around) plus 45 for the new engines; two new requirement kinds (`all`, `system`/`systems`). The Wardrobe moves into the Medals menu with 75 choices (16 more skins, 12 more trails, 9 more backgrounds, and new tap sparks and menu colours).
- **The Ascent** shows how much is open, each stage's progress, where you are, and a detail card for every system.
- **A permanent reset** in Settings behind three steps (what it does, are you sure, type RESET), with a backup kept first.
- **Buy amounts.** A new x5 everywhere; the Orbitals panel levels planets at any buy amount, closed-form.
- **Numbers that add up.** Engines (Stardust, Antimatter Mirror, Echo board) show what all of one kind make together and exactly what the next purchase adds at the buy amount, milestone jumps included.
- **The Merchant, tamed.** Three deals a ship; a deal taken stays sold; a new ship every 15 minutes of play (it refilled at once and docked again after every Supernova). A reroll swaps only the deals still for sale.
- **Music.** Seven composed songs, one per stage, each with an intro, verses, choruses and a bridge and a melody that changes every pass; Journey (the old default) moves between them by layer reached. Every evolve (higher with each tier), reset, medal, unlock, recipe, Planet Defense best and sigil has its own theme, with the soundtrack dipping underneath.
- **Looks.** Every upgrade, system and medal has its own symbol (a badge for what it does, or its milestone); every menu has its own backdrop and watermark; the star has a granulated, convecting surface, sunspots, flares and softer rays at every tier; planets come in 20 looks with atmospheres, night sides, rings and moonlets.
- **Update log** in Settings, and a one-time toast after an update.
- **Balance.** The new content is priced against the simulator so the game is no faster than 1.1.0; the third and fourth doublers cost about 400 of that engine's price, to deepen the late game rather than shorten the middle.
- **Speed.** Every speed budget passes, ahead of 1.1.0's numbers (late-game tick 1.6 ms, mid phone 52 fps, 8 h catch-up 250 ms, big trees 48 fps). Auto-planets buys levels in closed-form steps; automation makes one pass per tick (a pass buys all it can, so more only rebuilt the stats), and planets, trees, Belt, Constants and Supernova act once a real second (every other catch-up step); only a Supernova works out the Stardust rate for its next requirement; the Merchant docks no ships during a catch-up (one is waiting after); the minimized engine list builds no rows; and on a software renderer (no GPU) the sky draws at 1x.

## 1.1.0 (Phase 20: six new systems)
Built one at a time in the order planned, each checked against the code, tested and reviewed before the next. Save version 20 loads every 1.0.0 save (a real one is a test fixture); everything new starts at its default.
- **The Wardrobe** (10 medals). Seven star skins, five cursor trails and five backgrounds, each earned by a medal or a count, never bought. Photo Mode makes a 1,200 x 1,200 picture of your star with your best numbers.
- **Starlight Alchemy** (12 Supernovas). Put three ingredients from the Belt and the Starforge in the cauldron. A right mix finds one of 12 recipes; a wrong one makes Murky Sludge and an honest hint. Elixirs boost one thing for 5 to 15 minutes (stacking to an hour) and keep their time through any reset. Known recipes brew in bulk at the buy amount, and each card shows what its batch takes.
- **Space Weather** (first Supernova). Meteor Showers, Solar Flares, Stellar Wind, Eclipses, Nebula Storms and Auroras roll through every 6 to 12 minutes for 90 to 180 seconds, each with its own sky effect, HUD chip, toast and sound. Catch 25 comets for a Weather Beacon charge (up to 5) and summon the weather you want. Weather rolls the same online and offline.
- **Ship Customizer.** Every starship carries a hull, engine, cargo pod and scanner. Twelve new parts are bought once with alloy and fit any docked ship: matched hulls double loot in their sectors, engines and pods trade speed for loot, scanners steer rare finds. The Shipyard tab shows each ship's trip time and loot an hour in every sector, with Best fit and Recall. Ships without parts fly exactly as before.
- **Planet Defense** (3 Singularities). Place Lasers, Flak, Frost, Prism and Void Lances in twelve sockets around your planets and hold off void swarms wave by wave: armoured waves every 5th, a boss every 10th. The panel replays the very battle the game scored (x1, x2, x4 or skip). Every new best wave boosts planets and Stardust; from wave 10, auto-defend fights on its own, online and offline, and farms alloy when it can't push further. It never ends.
- **Hidden Sigils.** Seven secrets, one in each big tree. Own the two nodes beside one and it appears; buy it with that tree's currency. Each bends a rule: a big boost with a real cost, switched on and off at the Sigil Altar, kept for good.
- Ten new medals. With many boosts at once, the HUD folds the extras into a +N chip. A Meteor Shower or a Comet Brew brings the next comet at once.
- Found while building: a Supernova clears comet buffs, so elixir boosts are kept apart; elixir strengths, part effects and turret numbers all live in balance.ts; one turret could never pass wave 1 (the lanes end on three sides of the star), so sockets sit closer in and reach further.

## 1.0.0 (Phase 19: release)
- **Install it.** Starforge is an installable app: after one visit it plays offline. A new version never takes over by itself: a banner offers "Save and update", which saves first. Settings shows the version, an Install button where the browser offers one, and the credits.
- **Windows.** A Windows app built with Tauri: a small per-user installer, attached to each GitHub Release by a workflow that runs on version tags. Saves stay inside the app and are also written to a save file in AppData, which the game loads if the app's own storage is ever cleared. Version numbers and the app identifier are pinned by tests.
- **Accessibility.**
  - Tab stays inside an open panel, and Esc gives focus back to whatever opened it.
  - Every button has a name; the star taps from the keyboard.
  - All text keeps WCAG AA contrast (lighter red and slate for small text).
  - A new game follows the system's reduce-motion setting.
- **Speed budgets.** `npm run budgets` measures every budget on the Tech tab against the production build and a late-game save from the simulator. They all pass: 482 KB of code, a first load of 1.0 s on 4G, a 1.6 ms tick, 60 fps on desktop and 34 to 37 on a slowed phone, eight hours away caught up in under 250 ms, and the 200-node tree panning at 33 fps on a slowed phone. Getting there fixed:
  - The late-game tick took 14 ms. Eternity re-scanned whole trees for every repeat level it bought. At 40 times game speed, stat rebuilds, automation, rune rolls and wormhole reads ran every tick; they now run at most 4 or 5 times a real second and catch up on the game time in between.
  - Eight hours away took 26 s to catch up. Asteroids swept within a step are now counted in closed form, and the boot catch-up takes fewer, longer steps if it must, to finish within a quarter of a second (tests keep fixed steps).
  - Every automated reset played the full cinematic, a toast and a chord, several times a second late in the game. Automated resets are now quiet; milestones still get their toast.
  - Number punches forced a page layout each, and dozens of glowing buttons repainted every frame. Punches are now transform animations, and the glows pulse in opacity on their own layer.
  - Behind an open panel the sky is drawn every 8th frame (it's blurred and dimmed there), tree nodes redraw only when they change, and the Belt and Nursery look 5 times a second instead of 20.
- **Fixed:** late in the game, Element Codex samples and star seeds could pass 2^53, which the save validator refused, so the game silently stopped saving. Both now stop at 2^50, and the save now accepts counts of any size, so a counter that outgrows plain numbers loses precision instead of stopping saves.
- **Fixed** in a code review before release:
  - Star Quests now keep their progress through resets. Before, an engine, tier or Stardust quest open at a reset started over from where the reset left things.
  - Some mixes of Universe rules and Spark Hoards paid a fifth of a Spark less, lost to floating point.
  - A tab hidden for over a minute, late in the game, froze for seconds when shown again. It now catches up within the same time budget.
  - The Multiverse now counts as a reset for Eternity's "gains slowing" check.
  - Wiping the save in the Windows app also overwrites its save file, so the wiped game can't come back.
  - A panel no longer takes focus away from something already focused inside it.
- Void asteroids also give Dark Matter, from the first Singularity (the Game design's rarity table; they gave only their grade of ore). The Mutations panel no longer lists Wyrm and Nursery mutations as coming later: both arrived in Phase 10.
- New icon, drawn for the installable app and the Windows app.

## Phase 18: the Multiverse
- The Multiverse is the sixth and last reset. Ten Big Crunches in a universe pay 6 Multiverse Sparks (more from Spark Hoards, milestone 6 and the Universe Forge's rules). It resets every earlier layer; Trials, the Spire, the Leviathans, the Scale Tree, every collection, relics, the Codex, Mastery, the Starsmith Level, medals and Omni Constants stay.
  - Milestones: 1 Omni Constants and the ending; 2 the Forge takes 4 rules; 4 every rule; 6 +1 Spark; 10 every universe starts with all ten Eternity upgrades.
  - A rainbow cinematic: the sky cracks into coloured glass and shatters outwards, with a burst in every colour and its own sound. Reduced motion shows the glass glowing and fading instead.
- Spark choices: 114 choices at 0.2 Sparks each, bought in any order, on a radial tree with six arms (one per earlier layer).
  - Each arm's head stops the Multiverse resetting that layer; the next two keep its milestones and its Eternity automation (on the Crunch arm, Auto-Crunch).
  - The rest are boosts, six Spark Hoards (+0.2 Sparks each) and 24 echoes that level with no cap (0.2 x 2^level).
  - Sparks snap back to whole fifths after every change: in floating point 0.6 - 0.2 - 0.2 is 0.19999..., which could not buy the third choice.
- The Universe Forge: pick the next universe's rules (Dim, Dear, Cold, Heavy, Unlucky, Restless, Faint). Each makes that universe harder and pays from 15% to 50% more Sparks at its end. Two rules at once, four from Multiverse 2, all seven from Multiverse 4. Rules show in every stat breakdown as "Universe: <name>".
- Omni Constants: Omni Gain (Stardust), Omni Luck (Essence) and Omni Speed (Nova Shards). They never double, so they are linear and climb into the millions of levels; 10,000,000 levels buy in one step, for exactly the series' cost.
- The ending: the Last Starsmith's story opens by itself a moment after the first Multiverse (once no other panel is open), followed by the credits. Settings can show both again.
- Also:
  - A rainbow-rimmed Sparks pill in the top bar, and a rainbow Multiverse bar.
  - The Multiverse, Spark Choices, Universe Forge, story and credits panels, plus a confirm screen that names the next universe's rules.
  - Two icons, 5 medals, rails and the Ascent map.
  - The Big Crunch bar shrinks to an "AUTO" strip with Auto-Crunch.
- Fixed, found by the simulator's long runs: once passive shards had made a run's shards far larger than any single Supernova, while Stardust kept climbing, Eternity's Supernova rule (double the run, or a slowing gain) never fired. The Nebula waited on its 30 Supernovas, and Singularities and galaxies waited on the Nebula. Now, once a layer's reset is free (its "resets nothing" milestone one layer up), Eternity resets it as soon as it is ready. In a universe that keeps everything, Crunch runs fell from 2 to 4 hours to under 45 minutes.
- The simulator plays the Multiverse (SIM_MULTIVERSES). It spends Sparks (every layer's head first), buys Omni Constants, counts Crunches made by Auto-Crunch, never skips the last unexplored galaxy, and has a SIM_DEBUG_MV progress line. The first Multiverse comes at 12 h 24 min to 17 h 35 min across seeds (about twice the first Big Crunch). On seed 2026 the second universe takes 16 h and the third, keeping every layer, 4 h 39 min.
- Save version 19 (Spark choices, rules, the story flag; Big Crunches so far count towards the first universe), with a migration test that loads a real version 18 save.

## Phase 17: the Big Crunch, Eternity, the Echo boards, music and a polish pass
- The Big Crunch is the fifth reset. It needs 7 galaxies explored in the Crunch run, and two more for each Big Crunch already in this universe (up to all 20). With 7 every time, Eternity's automation and everything a Crunch keeps made the simulator's later Crunches minutes apart; with the step, the tenth comes at about twice the first (17 h 46 min against 8 h 29 min on the simulator's seed 2026), and the whole Galaxy Map gets used.
  - It gives floor((log10(GC / 10) + 1)^1.5) x Chronon gain, GC = Galaxy Cores gained this Crunch run. The plan divided by 1e6, which gives nothing: the simulator's first Crunch run gathers about 750 Cores (4 Chronons).
  - Lifetime Chronons multiply Galaxy Cores by (1 + Chronons)^0.5.
  - It resets the Galactic layer and everything below: Galaxy Cores, explored galaxies, the Galactic Tree and Galactic Constants (until milestones keep them). Megastructures, colonies, the Stardust Chart, Trials, the Spire, the Leviathans, wormholes and every collection stay. The Observatory draws a new sky.
  - Milestones: 1 keeps the Galactic milestones; 2 makes echo resets automatic; 3 keeps the Galactic Tree; 5 keeps Galactic Constants; 8 the Multiverse stirs; 10 a Galactic Rebirth resets nothing (1/50 of its gain trickles in).
  - The collapse cinematic: everything falls into a point, a white flash, then the universe bursts back out. Reduced motion shows a fade instead.
- The Echo boards replay the first three layers small. Five echo engines (on the shared engine kit) make Echo Dust; Echo Supernova trades the board for Echo Shards at 1e6 Echo Dust; Echo Nebula trades the shards for Echo Essence at 100 Echo Shards. Both go through the one reset function. Inside the boards, lifetime Echo Shards and Essence multiply echo output on a log scale; outside, lifetime echoes boost Stardust, shards, Essence and Chronons.
- Eternity: ten upgrades bought with Chronons (54 in all).
  - Five each automate a whole layer: Stardust (every auto-buyer from the start of a run, Stellar Constants), Supernova, Nebula, Singularity and Galactic (each layer's reset, tree, constants and side systems: shrines, the Black Hole, the Reactor, the Mirror, megastructures, colony buildings and research).
  - Each automated layer has a switch and a rule: reset when the gain at least doubles this run's total, or at a number you set. Automation never cuts an unfinished Trial short, waits until the galaxy you're in is explored, never auto-rolls the Eternal Shrine, and spends at most a tenth of a currency on anything that repeats for ever.
  - The rest are boosts: Chronons x2, echo output x10, the game 25% faster, Galaxy Cores x2, and False Dawn (every Crunch run starts with three galaxies explored).
  - Eternal Constants: Entropy (more Chronons) and Recursion (echo output).
- Music: a soundtrack synthesised live in WebAudio, with a pad from the start and one instrument for each layer reached (arpeggio, bass, percussion, bells, choir). Three soundtracks (Starlight, Deep Void, Nebula) or off, a music volume in Settings, paused while the tab is hidden.
- The polish pass (every change is listed in POLISH.md):
  - Each reset flashes, rings and bursts in its own layer's colour, with its own sound.
  - A purchase punches its row and flashes the price green; an unlock breaks the rail button's padlock; the rune roll card spins a reel for about a second before landing.
  - New sounds for runes, auras, hatching, cards, Black Hole stages, shrine stars, smelting, mutations and big news.
  - Automated purchases no longer chime several times a second.
  - Text says "2 Singularities", "5 galaxies" and "3 Big Crunches" (it said "Singularitys"); the welcome-back report names layers properly.
  - Constants bought with Dark Matter, Galaxy Cores or Chronons show their own currency's icon (they showed Stardust).
  - Late in the game the right-hand system grid scrolls instead of squeezing the engine shop.
  - Reset bars of layers Eternity runs shrink to a slim "AUTO" strip.
- Fixed: from Singularity milestone 10 ("a Nebula resets nothing"), an auto-Nebula paid its full gain every tick, because the run's shards never started over. A Nebula, a Singularity and a Galactic Rebirth now always start their gain's run total over, as the Supernova already did.
- Fixed, found by the simulator's long runs:
  - The first Echo boards fed themselves: shards and Essence multiplied echo output by their full amount, so each cycle's output grew with the last cycle's dust and reached e235,000 in minutes (and through the real-game boosts, everything else). Their boosts inside the boards are now log-scale.
  - Starships delivered trips one at a time, up to 1,000 a tick. Late in the game a trip lasts a fraction of a tick, so ships did tens of thousands of deliveries a second and dropped the rest. Every trip that ends in a tick now pays at once, with rare finds as one draw (like the runes), so a million trips cost the same as one.
  - Max buying corrected its estimate one purchase at a time, which could take millions of steps at enormous numbers. It now searches in halves: at most about 50 checks.
  - The Void and Galactic rune shrines cost the currency they boost, and a levelled shrine multiplies by e29: Galaxy Cores reached e78 in one Crunch run. In the late shrines, Dark Matter, Galaxy Core and Chronon multipliers are now damped (at most about x800, x28 and x4).
  - Expansion, Arms and Lattice raise multipliers to a power, and are bought with the currency those multipliers grow, so the exponent itself compounded (e213 Galaxy Cores). Like Reactor strength, they now stop at x3, x2 and x2.
- Also: the Chronons pill, the Big Crunch bar, three panels and a confirm screen, five icons, 5 medals, rails and the Ascent map (with the Multiverse teaser).
- The simulator plays the Big Crunch (SIM_CRUNCHES), buys Eternity and plays the Echo boards.
- Save version 18, with a migration test that loads a real version 17 save.

## Phase 16: Megastructures, Colonies and Terraforming
- Six megastructures of five stages: Dyson Swarm, Ringworld, Matrioshka Brain, Star Lifter, Stellar Engine and Birch Planet.
  - They open from 1 to 5 Galactic Rebirths.
  - A stage costs a bundle of currencies from every layer, each 10^(base + step x stage).
  - Every stage's reward is permanent: no reset touches what you've built, and it shows in the breakdowns as "<name> (megastructure)".
  - Built stages are drawn round the star: Dyson panels on an orbit, the Ringworld's band, Matrioshka shells, Star Lifter jets, the Stellar Engine's mirror, and the Birch Planet far behind.
  - Prices were set from the currencies the simulator saw at each Galactic Rebirth. A first draft let the Matrioshka Brain finish at once (its Essence prices were far below late-game Essence), which pushed Galaxy Cores up tenfold.
- Colonies open at 2 Galactic Rebirths, with one per galaxy explored plus one (up to 8), founded with Galaxy Cores (5 x 3^n).
  - Five planet types each boost one thing by x(1 + log10(1 + people))^power.
  - People grow logistically towards capacity. The tick uses the exact solution, so an hour away, an hour in one step and 7,200 half-second steps end at the same population (tested).
  - Colonies make Supply for buildings (Housing, Farms, Factories), spaceports, trade routes (x1.5 Supply at both ends) and six research projects.
- Terraforming (after the Terraformers research): paint a colony planet's 19 hexes with four biomes; neighbouring pairs score together, and some clash.
  - Finishing locks the score in: the colony's bonus is multiplied by (1 + score / 10) for good.
- Galactic Constants Halo (colony capacity) and Lattice (megastructure rewards).
- The game's libraries (Pixi, React, the number and save libraries) now load as their own cached chunks. The game's own code is 578 kB instead of a single 1.21 MB file.
- Also: three panels, three icons, 5 medals, rails and the Ascent map.
- The simulator builds megastructure stages, founds and develops colonies, opens routes, researches and terraforms. It logs every currency's size at each Galactic Rebirth with SIM_DEBUG_MAG.
- Save version 17, with a migration test that loads a real version 16 save.

## Phase 15: Galactic Rebirth
- Galactic Rebirth is the fourth reset. It needs all 8 Black Hole stages, the Mirror Key and 1e8 Dark Matter gained in the Galactic run.
  - It gives floor((DM / 1e8)^0.5) x Galaxy Core gain. The plan's ^0.25 gave 3 to 6 Cores a run in the simulator, which bought 18 of the 200 tree nodes in four Galactic Rebirths.
  - Lifetime Galaxy Cores multiply Dark Matter by (1 + GC)^0.3.
  - It resets the Singularity layer: Dark Matter, the Singularity Tree, Void Constants, the Black Hole (with the Prism Wheel banks and the Reactor), the Antimatter Mirror (its key stays) and everything below.
  - It keeps collections, Trials, the Spire, the Leviathans and the Wormhole Network.
- Milestones:
  - 1: keep the Singularity milestones;
  - 2: auto-Singularity at a gain you set;
  - 3: keep the Singularity Tree;
  - 5: keep the Black Hole;
  - 7: the Big Crunch stirs;
  - 10: a Singularity resets nothing (1/50 of its gain trickles in).
- 20 galaxies: Milky Way, Spiral, Elliptical, Ring, Dwarf, Quasar Host and fourteen more.
  - Before each Galactic Rebirth, pick where you restart on the Galaxy Map, up to four galaxies beyond those you've explored.
  - A galaxy's rule is only stat sources ("Galaxy: <name>" in every breakdown) and holds only while you're there.
  - Its goal is an amount to reach there (Singularities, Dark Matter, anti-star collapses or Essence). Meeting it explores the galaxy, and its reward stays for good.
  - The galaxy you're in tints the sky. The Index's Galaxies page is live.
- The Galactic Tree: 200 nodes on five spiral arms (Active, Idle, Runes, Wyrms, Production), five capstones where neighbouring arms end, and four echoes with no cap. It runs on the shared tree engine and stays smooth at phone size.
- Galactic Constants: Expansion (cleared galaxies' rewards grow stronger) and Arms (the Galactic Tree's multipliers grow stronger, through a new power hook in the tree engine).
- The Observatory: each Galactic run rolls a new sky of 24 stars from a seed saved with the game, each with a mass, a temperature and a distance.
  - Link stars into a constellation (8 links). Long links between heavy, near stars score most, and every closed shape adds 25%. The score depends only on which pairs are linked.
  - Recording a better score on this sky pays the difference in Star Charts, which buy the 12-node Stardust Chart.
- Long-run fixes the Galactic simulator found (it plays 7 Galactic Rebirths, almost 10 hours):
  - Relic effects were plain numbers and overflowed to Infinity past about 3,000 levels, poisoning every stat after them. They are now Decimals.
  - Merchant relic deals restock as you buy and cost almost nothing late on, so relics could be pushed to tens of thousands of levels. Relics now stop at 1,000 levels, and growth past level 300 is x1.02 a level instead of x1.25. Normal play reaches about 260 levels by the Nebula era and is unchanged.
  - Comet Fusion's speed upgrade stops at 60 levels: past that, 1.12^level overflowed and spawning would have divided by zero.
  - Reset toasts name Dark Matter and Galaxy Cores properly (the Singularity's had shown "darkMatter").
- Also: a Galactic reset bar, a Galaxy Cores pill, six panels (Galactic Rebirth, its confirm screen, the Galactic Tree, the Galaxy Map, the Observatory, the Stardust Chart), two icons, 3 medals and the Ascent map.
- The simulator plays on through Galactic Rebirths with SIM_GALACTICS. It picks the first unexplored galaxy, stays until it's explored, spends Galaxy Cores, and draws a greedy constellation.

## Phase 2: Saves, offline earnings and variety
