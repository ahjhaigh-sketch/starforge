# Changelog

## Phase 8: the Nebula and Constellation Runes
- The Nebula, the second reset, needs 30 Supernovas and 100,000 shards gathered in one Nebula run.
  - Essence = floor((shards this run / 100,000)^0.35); the first Nebula always gives 2.
  - Lifetime Essence multiplies shard gain by (1 + Essence)^0.4.
  - A Nebula bar sits under the Supernova bar. A confirm screen lists what resets (Stardust, the Supernova layer, shards) and what stays (relics, the Codex and runes).
  - Supernova counts are now per run, lifetime and best, so milestones keep working across Nebulas.
- Nebula milestones remove chores:
  - 1: keep Supernova milestones;
  - 2: auto-Supernova at a shard amount you type;
  - 3: Void Wyrms appear on the rails;
  - 5: keep the Nova Tree;
  - 8: the Singularity appears;
  - 10: a Supernova resets nothing and 1/50 of its gain trickles in every second.
- The Nebula Tree: 100 nodes in a drifting cloud, drawn with the same tree view as the Nova Tree.
  - A root, four wisps of 22 nodes (Veil, Bloom, Drift, Lattice), four capstones and seven repeatable echoes.
  - Nodes cost Essence, 1 x 2.6^depth along a wisp.
- Nebula Constants (infinite upgrades, bought with Essence): Rune Speed, Rune Bulk, Resonance (rune effects) and Drift (Essence).
- Constellation Runes: eight shrines, each with its own currency.
  - Dust and Nova open at the first Nebula, Nebula and Forge at the second, Wyrm with the first Wyrm. Void, Galactic and Eternal wait on the rails for later layers.
  - Each shrine has 19 runes from 1 in 2 to 1 in e36, a hidden "???" rune and an Anomaly that ignores luck.
  - Effective odds use Rune Luck and Luck Root in log space, never better than 1 in 10.
  - Every 10th roll gets x2 luck, and the rarest visible rune has a hard pity at 3 x its odds.
  - Rune level = min(cap, floor(sqrt(copies))); each rune boosts 1 to 8 stats, shown as one "Runes: <shrine>" line per stat in the breakdowns.
- Rolling:
  - Runes per second = speed (up to 20 rolls a second) x bulk.
  - Every rune costs the shrine's price, so spend per second is price x runes per second.
  - Auto-roll slows to what you can afford and never goes negative; a slider caps the roll rate.
  - Hand rolls, a keep filter for auto-roll, and "You can roll for" (∞ when income covers it).
  - The engine never loops roll by roll: expected hits per tick, Poisson draws below 1,000. A billion billion runes a second costs the computer the same as one.
  - Auto-roll keeps going offline from its own random stream.
- A slot-machine card lands for each roll ("Nothing this time" when a roll finds no new copies), and Mythic or rarer finds get a banner across the screen. Both respect reduced motion.
- The hidden Dust rune, the Whisper of Sylthaz, opens a secret Starsmith perk.
- Nebula clouds drift through the starfield once you have gone Nebula, with an Essence pill in the top bar.
- 8 new medals for Nebulas, runes found and runes rolled.
- Balance fixes found by the simulator, which now plays to 8 Nebulas:
  - Resonance is +2% rune effects a level with no doublings, and it never scales a rune's power. A doubling Resonance was multiplied into every rune of a shrine and ran away.
  - Merchant Luck stops at level 25. Relic deals give 1, 2 or 5 levels, and every deal is sized from rates, never lifetime totals.
  - Nebula Tree echoes cost x4 to x10 more a level (they were x3), so each Nebula adds a little less than the last.
  - Result: across five seeds, Nebulas 2 to 8 take 2.2 to 3.5 times the bot time of the first Nebula.
- Save version 9, with a migration test that loads a real version 8 save.

## Phase 7: Supernova extras
- Comet Fusion opens at 6 Supernovas.
  - A merge board: 3x3, growing to 5x5. A comet arrives every 12 s.
  - Drag a comet onto one of the same level (or tap one, then the other) and exactly three of that level fuse into the next.
  - Your best level ever multiplies Stardust and taps (x1.25 a level) and comet strength.
  - Upgrades (shards, with buy amounts): comet lure, hot start, wider board, and Auto-fuse, which keeps fusing offline.
- Star Pulse Attunements open at 8 Supernovas, with up to 3 equipped on the Star Core.
  - **Pulse:** a ring closes on the star each beat. The first tap in a beat's window is graded x25, x5 or x2, and off-beat taps give nothing, so mashing can't beat rhythm. The average grade is a tap-power bonus in the breakdown.
  - **Heartbeat:** a meter that taps fill and time drains; production up to x4, but at 100 it overloads to zero.
  - **Chaos Die:** one of six effects every 20 taps.
  - **Gravity Lens:** taps x6, at most 4 a second.
  - Chips on the Star Core show each one's state, and "Perfect!" floats up.
- The Wandering Merchant opens at 10 Supernovas.
  - Four deals sized from your economy the moment they appear: shards, ore, alloy, relic levels, Codex samples, XP or a Frenzy.
  - A taken deal is replaced at once, and a new ship docks after every Supernova.
  - Rerolls cost shards. Merchant Luck makes deals bigger. Rare deals are x3 and the Mythical Merchant is x100, with a toast when it docks.
- Starship Expeditions open at 15 Supernovas.
  - Up to 5 ships fly to 6 named sectors, opened by your best Star Tier.
  - Trip time is distance / speed, so Engines shorten every trip; farther sectors pay more per hour.
  - Cargo and Crew upgrades cost Gold and Crystal alloy, giving the rarer grades a use.
  - Ships on repeat keep flying offline. Rare finds are relic levels or Codex samples.
- All four are kept through Supernova and sit on the rails before they open. Fusion, the Chaos Die, the Merchant and the ships each roll from their own random stream.
- Relic levels from deals and rare finds respect each relic's level cap.
- 11 new medals for fusion, perfect Pulse taps, Chaos rolls, deals and trips.
- The simulator bot fuses comets, uses the Gravity Lens, takes good deals and keeps ships flying. The milestone targets hold.
- Save version 8, with a migration test that loads a real version 7 save.

## Phase 6: Starforge
- The Starforge opens at 3 Supernovas, in one panel with three tabs: Furnace, Relics and Codex.
- Ore grades: common ore makes Iron alloy. A rare asteroid also drops as much of its own grade's ore (Gold, Crystal, Prismatic, Void) as its common ore, so every grade arrives at about a tenth of iron's rate.
- The furnace, drawn in PixiJS: ore fills the hopper, lumps ride the Mag-Belt into the glowing Stellar Furnace, and ingots slide out onto a stack.
  - 100 ore makes 1 alloy, with a 10 s base batch.
  - Upgrades (all with buy amounts): Mag-Belt speed (x1.1), Bulk smelting (+1 batch) and Crucibles (up to 6 batches at once).
  - Pick a grade; switch it on or off.
  - The hopper takes all, half (the default) or a tenth of newly mined common ore, so Belt upgrades and drones keep the rest.
  - Each batch keeps the ore it took and the alloy it will give, so reloading mid-smelt loses nothing.
  - Away, it runs at the offline efficiency, and one long catch-up step gives what short ticks would.
- 50 relics, 10 per alloy grade.
  - Crafting costs 10 alloy; level L costs 10 x 1.6^L, with buy amounts.
  - Effect = base x 1.25^(level - 1), through the stat pipeline, so relics show in every stat breakdown.
  - 6 equip slots, plus 1 from iron in the Codex. Crafting every relic of a grade doubles that alloy. Relics on capped stats stop at level 10.
- The Element Codex: all 118 elements on a periodic table.
  - Each Supernova grants floor(1 + log10(S / 1e7)) samples of every element the star could fuse at the tier it reached: Blue Giant up to oxygen, Neutron Star up to iron, Quasar barium, Blazar uranium, White Hole everything.
  - Element level is floor(log2(samples)) + 1. Each family gives a perk per level, and a completed row gives x1.5.
  - Helium adds an engine milestone, iron a relic slot, gold doubles Gold alloy, and uranium raises shard gain.
  - The Supernova confirm screen previews the samples you'll get, and toasts announce new elements and completed rows.
- Drill bits are crafted from Iron alloy, as the plan says.
- Fixed from Phase 5: Belt upgrades and drill drones and bits now follow the buy amount (x1 to Max, Next, typed), as rule 8 requires.
- 24 new medals: the Belt, Deep Core, Starsmith Level, smelting, relics and elements.
- The Stats screen shows shard, ore, XP and alloy gain, smelting speed and drilling, plus counters for the new systems. The welcome-back screen names every ore and alloy.
- Toasts:
  - A new tier and a Supernova are priority toasts: smaller news can no longer push them off the screen.
  - Five or more unlocks at once get a short title.
- Offline efficiency is capped at 100%, however many relics and nodes stack up.
- Balance simulator:
  - The bot now sweeps the Belt, tiers up, spends perks, smelts and crafts relics every 5 seconds.
  - It found the Codex halving early runs, so samples start when the Starforge opens and helium's milestone sits at 1,250 owned.
- Save version 7, with a migration test that loads a real version 6 save.

## Phase 5: Asteroid Belt
- The Asteroid Belt opens at Red Dwarf.
  - A PixiJS harvest field: asteroids drift in up to the field's cap. A tractor-beam circle follows the mouse, a dragging finger, or the arrow keys.
  - Sweeping harvests ore, Starsmith XP and a little Stardust. A quick flick sweeps everything along its path, not only where the beam lands each frame.
  - Five rarities: Iron, Gold 1 in 50 (x10), Crystal 1 in 400 (x50), Prismatic 1 in 5,000 (x500), Void 1 in 100,000 (x10,000). Rare ones glow, burst and chime; Prismatic and Void get a toast.
  - Rarities come from the Belt's own seeded random stream. The field is saved, so reloading never re-rolls an asteroid, and the Belt never shifts the rolls of comets or crits.
- Belt upgrades: Value, Amount, Growth, Beam (8 levels) and XP, priced as on the Math and balance tab.
- Belt Tiers: 100 x 3^(T^1.2) ore gathered this tier.
  - Each tier is a mini-reset of ore and upgrades. It gives x2 ore and XP, plus one automation: a drone, faster drones, passive ore, free upgrades, a second drone, faster drones again.
- The whole Belt, Deep Core and Starsmith Level are kept through Supernova.
- Drones and passive ore keep working offline at the offline efficiency. The Belt settles correctly in one 43-second catch-up step, so a day away harvests what a day played would.
- Deep Core Drilling opens at 5 Supernovas.
  - Click the core for 2 seconds of drilling. Drill drones add drilling, drill bits multiply it, and it drills on its own (offline too).
  - Cores have 100 x 8^depth HP and break into ore. Four depth layers (Crust, Mantle, Core, Singularity Seam), each with its own ore and mastery, which speeds up drilling there.
- Starsmith Level: XP from taps, engines, evolving, quests, asteroids and Supernovas.
  - Level L needs 50 x 2.7^(L^0.75) XP.
  - Every level is a perk point: Prospector, Wide Belt, Gravity Lure, Scholar, Long Beam. Resetting perks is free, any time.
  - A level chip with an XP bar sits in the top bar and opens the Level panel.
- Ore pill in the top bar.
- Unlocks that arrive together share one toast. Systems opened by a new Star Tier are already named in that tier's toast, so it no longer gets pushed off a phone screen.
- The welcome-back screen names Ore, Starsmith XP and Nova Shards properly.
- Balance simulator:
  - The bot's old rule ("Supernova when it doubles lifetime shards") could stall for hours once passive shards arrive at 20 Supernovas. From 20 on it now resets when shards per second peak.
  - A seed can be set (`SIM_SEED`).
- Save version 6, with a migration test that loads a real version 5 save.
- New script `scripts/soak.mjs`: plays the built game for 2 minutes at desktop and phone size and fails on any console error.

## Phase 4: Supernova (the version for friends)
- One generic reset system (`engine/layers.ts`).
  - A layer is data: currency, readiness, gain, the parts it resets (each with what can keep it), what is always kept, and milestones.
  - One function performs every reset. It is deterministic, decides what is kept before changing anything, and clears every cached stat.
- Supernova, the first layer.
  - Needs a Blue Giant and 10M Stardust gathered this run. The first gives exactly 3 shards; after that floor((S / 1e7)^0.4), softcapped above 10,000.
  - Lifetime shards boost every Stardust gain, so spending them never weakens you.
  - The bottom bar comes alive when ready, showing "+N" and "next shard at X".
  - A confirm screen (switchable in Settings) lists what resets and what is kept, straight from the reset function.
  - The cinematic: a flash in the layer colour, a shockwave ring, screen shake, 140 particles and the star collapsing and being reborn. Reduced motion and reduced flashing are respected.
  - The run saves straight away.
- Milestones:
  - 1: the Nova Tree.
  - 2: Auto-Evolve.
  - 3: the Starforge (built in Phase 6) and the Spark Mote auto-buyer.
  - 5: auto-buyers for engines 2 to 5.
  - 8: start 2 tiers below your best.
  - 12: auto-buyers for everything.
  - 20: 1% of your Supernova gain every second.
  - 30: the Nebula appears.
- The Nova Tree: 79 nodes on a three-arm spiral.
  - Arms: Forge-heart (engines), Starstrike (taps and comets), Dreaming (offline, shards, automation).
  - Capstones where arms meet, and six repeatable outer nodes with no cap.
  - The first node of each arm is an escalating pick.
  - Colours mean something (unlock, add, multiply, exponent, change a rule, pick).
- A reusable tree view built for big trees.
  - Drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom, + and -, arrow keys between nodes, Enter to buy.
  - A minimap you can click, search by name, "Next to buy", and reveal-as-you-buy with fog one step ahead.
  - Pan and zoom never re-render React. Tested at 200 nodes.
- The Automation hub: every auto-buyer with an on/off switch and a spending limit (all, half, a tenth). They run in the normal tick, so they keep working offline.
- The Ascent: every system as a deck on a vertical star map, Protostar at the bottom, with what the next ones need. Ascent sits at the top of the left rail.
- Balance simulator (`npm run sim`). Its findings:
  - Fixed: the stat cache wasn't cleared by resets.
  - Fixed: "% of Stardust/s per tap" was being multiplied by every tap multiplier. It's now added after them, as the plan says.
  - Fixed: Star Quests could be chain-claimed for 3 minutes of production each right after a reset. Now 30 s, capped at half the run's Stardust.
  - Tuned: shard gain ^0.4, glow ^0.3, constant growth 1.3 to 1.35, tree prices 3 x 7^depth.
  - Result: Nebula at 30 Supernovas, with about 73% of the tree bought.
- Save version 5, with a migration test that loads a real version 4 save.
- Tests:
  - Resets are deterministic.
  - The confirm screen's kept list matches what is kept.
  - The first Supernova gives exactly 3; "next shard at" is exact; the lifetime glow works.
  - Tree prices, picks, repeatables and capstones.
  - Auto-buyers obey their switches, including offline.
  - Browser tests: reaching a first Supernova with only the on-screen buttons (on phone too), the tree at 200 nodes with mouse, keyboard and touch, and the automation switches.

## Phase 3: Early depth
- Cosmic Constants: one reusable infinite-upgrade module.
  - No level cap. Cost is base x g^L, with growth stepping to g^1.1 after Lv 1,000 and g^1.2 after Lv 100,000.
  - Effects are (1 + a x L), doubled every 25 levels to 1,000, then every 100 to 100,000, then every 1,000 (1,930 doublings at a million levels).
  - Bulk cost and Max are closed-form per bracket, so buying a million levels takes under 20 ms.
  - The first set (Stellar) opens at Golden Sun: Gravity (engines), Light (taps), Heat (Lucky comets and buff length), and Orbit (planets) from Blue Giant.
  - The panel previews each effect now and after the purchase.
- A new "Next" buy amount everywhere: up to an engine's next x2 milestone, or a constant's next doubling. Hotkey 6.
- Engine Mastery from Red Supergiant: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum and Cosmic ranks per engine from engines ever bought, each x1.5 for that engine and never lost. Rank pips show on the shop tiles.
- Star Quests from Golden Sun: a board of 3 seeded quests (taps, engines, Stardust, owning more of your best engine, comets, crits, an upgrade, evolving). Each is scaled to your progress and measured from when it appeared. Claiming pays 3 minutes of production, adds +1% production forever, and a new quest takes its place at once.
- Orbitals from Blue Giant:
  - 4 rings (Blue Giant, Red Supergiant, Magnetar, Blazar) with 4, 6, 8 and 10 slots.
  - 5 planet types, each boosting 3 engines by +25% a level, plus +20% for each neighbour of a different type.
  - Changing a planet's type is free.
  - The rings and planets are drawn around the star, passing in front of and behind it.
- A red "!" on rail buttons when something there can be bought or claimed.
- Save version 4, with a migration test that loads a real version 3 save.
- Tests:
  - A million levels cost exactly the three-bracket series sum.
  - Max never overspends across every bracket (property test).
  - The doubling schedule; Mastery surviving a run wipe and reload; quest scaling, claiming and refilling; planet prices, neighbours and free type changes.
  - A browser test that buys a constant, places a planet and claims a quest.

## Phase 2: Saves, offline earnings and variety
- Welcome back: any time away over a minute is simulated in at most 2,000 steps. A progress bar shows if that takes a moment, then a "Welcome back" screen shows time away, time counted, efficiency and gains. Offline starts at 2 hours and 50%.
- Backups: a rolling backup in the browser database every 5 minutes of play and before catching up after 10+ minutes away (10 kept). Settings lists them with a two-step Restore. If both save slots are unreadable, the newest readable backup loads.
- Import shows a preview (tier, Stardust, playtime, save date) before loading and refuses junk with the reason. Imports and restores back up the current game first.
- Comets from Red Dwarf: one every 60 to 120 s (sooner with upgrades), catchable for 9 s by click or the C key. Frenzy (x7 engines, 30 s, 60%), Lucky (15% of your Stardust, at most 15 minutes of production, 30%), Tapstorm (x50 taps, 10 s, 9%), Wish (engines half price, 30 s, 1%). Buff chips in the top bar. Buffs run on game time, so offline and online agree, and no comets spawn during catch-up.
- 60 upgrades:
  - 30 engine doublers and 10 tap upgrades (+1, per-mote, x2, a share of Stardust/s, crits, combo).
  - 6 production, 6 engine-synergy, 5 comet and 3 offline upgrades.
  - Available ones sit as glowing tiles above the shop; the Upgrades panel shows every one, bought, available or locked with its requirement.
  - Tooltips say when each effect applies.
- 102 medals, each +1% Stardust. Five Goals of 10 tiers each (taps, comets, engines, crits, Stardust), each tier +2% Rune Luck, x1.05 Rune Bulk and +1% Stardust. Medal toasts arrive in batches.
- Stats screen: every stat broken down by source in pipeline order, plus all-time totals.
- Settings: autosave interval (5/15/30/60 s), mute, master and effects volume, ask before resets, pop-up messages, and hotkeys (T tap, E evolve, 1 to 5 buy amounts, C catch, M mute).
- Sound effects made in code: taps (brighter on crits), purchases rising in pitch, tier-up chord, comet whoosh and catch, medal and unlock chimes.
- Save version 3, with a migration test that loads a real version 2 save. Medals already earned are granted on first load.
- Tests:
  - Offline equivalence: 1 h offline matches 1 h played within 1%.
  - Every medal unlocks exactly once.
  - Comet odds, buffs, export/import identity, junk import and upgrade effects.
  - Browser tests: welcome back after 2 hours (including the backup), a purchase surviving a closed tab, junk import, one panel at a time, catching a comet, and buying an upgrade tile.

## Phase 1: The Star, Engines and Tiers
- The Star Core is a PixiJS shader: shaded sphere, churning plasma, corona rays, ink outline and gloss, with a look per tier (Pulsar beams, Quasar and Blazar jets, rainbow fringes on Magnetar and White Hole). It squashes on every tap and eases into each new tier.
- Sky: three depths of drifting, twinkling stars. A pool of 400 particles drives every burst. Spark Motes orbit the star, passing in front of and behind it.
- Tapping: floating "+N" numbers, crits from Golden Sun (5% for x10), a combo meter (x1.5, x2, x3 at 10, 25 and 50 taps in a row), keyboard tapping (Space or Enter on the star), and a cap of 30 taps a second.
- 15 engines with x1.15 costs (x1.25 past 200 owned), output doubling at 12 milestones, and "???" rows until you own the engine before. Buying supports x1, x10, x100, x1K, Max and a typed amount, and every row previews its total cost. On phones the shop is a drawer.
- 12 Star Tiers from Protostar to White Hole, priced 1,000 x 50^(k - 2). Each one is x2 everything, plays a burst and a toast, and names what it unlocks.
- Teaser rails: the left rail holds this stage's systems; a "Coming up" stack under the shop holds later layers. Each locked system shows a padlock, its requirement and a progress ring; further ones are "???" silhouettes. Unlocks are permanent once earned. The Supernova bar shows its requirement.
- 17 new chunky SVG icons.
- Save version 2, with a migration test that loads a real version 1 save.
- Tests: bracketed cost maths, a property test that Max never overspends or comes up short, 100 taps = 100 x tap power, combo steps, the tap cap, crit rate, milestones, tier costs, unlocks, and browser tests for tapping, buying, evolving, buy amounts and rail tooltips. 60 fps with 400 particles at both sizes.

## Phase 0: Foundations
- Vite 8 + React 19 + TypeScript 6 project with pinned versions; strict type checking.
- Engine core: Decimal helpers (bulk cost, max affordable, softcap), game state, stat pipeline (add, multiply, power, softcap) with breakdowns, event bus, seeded RNG saved with the game, fixed 20 Hz real-clock loop, offline/hidden-tab catch-up in at most 2,000 steps.

- Dev panel (`?dev=1`), GitHub Pages deploy workflow, unit tests and browser smoke tests.
